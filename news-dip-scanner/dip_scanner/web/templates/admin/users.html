What these do

Reset link (or Setup link for an account without a password): a link to choose a password, valid once for 48 hours. It is shown to you once, to send to the person yourself; using it signs them out everywhere.

Disable: signs the person out at once; they can't sign in and get no alerts until the account is enabled again, and the unused invite and password links they made stop working for good. Nothing of theirs is deleted.

Make admin: admins see these pages, invite people and control the scanner. The last admin who can sign in can't be made a member or disabled. An admin made a member loses the unused links they made, too.
