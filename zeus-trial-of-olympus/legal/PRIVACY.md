# Privacy Policy — Zeus Trials (Greek Devvit Games)

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

Zeus Trials is a game that runs inside Reddit using Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit).

## What we collect
When you play, the app stores, against your **Reddit username**:
- your best score overall and per level, and the highest level you have unlocked;
- your score on the daily leaderboard for the current day (kept for about 14 days).

Your browser may also store your best score, unlocked level and sound setting in local storage on your device.

## What we do not collect
We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, contacts or any payment information.
We do not use advertising or analytics, and the app does not load scripts, fonts or media from third-party sites.

## How data is stored and shared
Scores are stored in Reddit's Devvit storage for this app, on Reddit's infrastructure. Your username and daily score
may be shown to other players on the leaderboard. We do not sell data or share it with third parties.
Reddit's own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Reddit.

## Deleting your data
To have your stored scores removed, open an issue at https://github.com/venetisgr/work_stuff/issues with your Reddit username.
Daily leaderboard entries expire automatically.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
